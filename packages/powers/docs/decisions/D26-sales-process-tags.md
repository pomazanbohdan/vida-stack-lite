# D26 — Sales process tags for the H1 predictable-revenue batch

Date: 2026-11-04
Status: accepted

## Context

The H1 source-admission panel is applying `predictable-revenue`. The source
defines a repeatable B2B sales process with separated SDR, AE and CSM roles,
three lead types, outbound referral email cadence, and pipeline math. The
existing business patterns describe marketing and persuasion, not sales-role
design or outbound pipeline generation. No capability described designing a
sales process.

## Decision

Add one capability and three pattern tags with explicit definitions to
`catalog.tag_dictionary`:

| Dimension | Tag | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| capability | `design-sales-motion` | Ability to design a repeatable sales motion with separated roles, lead types and handoffs. |
| pattern | `sales-role-specialization` | An approach in which prospecting, closing and retention are separate roles with their own metrics and handoffs. |
| pattern | `outbound-prospecting` | An approach in which outbound pipeline is generated by a dedicated SDR through personalized referral emails and a follow-up cadence. |
| pattern | `pipeline-math` | An approach in which the revenue goal is worked backward into deals, opportunities, prospects and emails. |

The tags are metadata only. They do not grant permission, acceptance or
admission; D18 review remains required for every file.

## Application

`predictable-revenue` uses `design-sales-motion` with
`sales-role-specialization` for the overview, role separation, SDR/AE/CSM
definitions, hiring, ramp and compensation; `outbound-prospecting` for Cold
Calling 2.0, the referral email and the follow-up cadence; `pipeline-math` for
the revenue model; `customer-avatar` for the ICP; and the existing
`channel-measurement`, `nurture-sequence`, `lifetime-value`, `sales-conversion`,
`metric-gated-decision`, `scoring-rubric`, `mistake-list` and
`diagnostic-questions` for the remaining rules. Already-applied files keep their
existing metadata; no retroactive retag is performed by this decision.

## Consequences

`catalog.json` revision advances by the four tag entries. The corpus, provenance
and instruction bodies are unchanged. The tag dictionary remains the only
vocabulary owner; `check-corpus` validates every new tag against its definition.
