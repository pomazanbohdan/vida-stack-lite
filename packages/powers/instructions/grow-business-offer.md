# Make the offer impossible to refuse

Phase 2 constructs a Grand Slam Offer before traffic. Apply Value = (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood) / (Time Delay x Effort and Sacrifice), increasing the numerator and reducing the denominator. Combine core product, named objection-killing bonuses, risk-reversing guarantee and ethical real scarcity until the stack dwarfs price; name it with MAGIC (Magnetic reason, Avatar, Goal, time Indicator, Container). Premium pricing is intended to attract committed customers who churn less. Ask whether 'too expensive' reflects price or value construction, whether to raise price/add done-for-you bonus or hold, and which three bonuses ship. Create/extend OFFER.md with offer stack (honest value and objection killed) and price metric. Done only with scored offer, named bonuses, guarantee, real scarcity, MAGIC name and recorded pricing decision.
