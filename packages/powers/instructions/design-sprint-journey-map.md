# Map the customer journey and expert insights

Map the customer journey by listing customer-type actors and drawing a left-to-right journey in 5-15 steps. Interview teammates with specialized knowledge and capture notes on the whiteboard. Rephrase problems as How Might We opportunities, write one per sticky note, then vote and organize the best on the map.
