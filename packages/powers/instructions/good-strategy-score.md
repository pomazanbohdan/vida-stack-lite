# Score the strategy document

Score strategy documents against the eight Quick Diagnostic rows, count passes, report the score and changes needed for 10/10, and use the supplied bands. The 9–10 band requires a complete kernel, pivot-point focus, and an explicit no-list; lower bands describe the specified gaps.
