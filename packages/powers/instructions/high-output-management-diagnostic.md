# Run the management diagnostic

Use the diagnostic questions to check whether team output can be stated in one sentence; every quantity metric has a quality pair; the limiting step is known; reports set their 1:1 agendas; 1:1 frequency follows TRM (weekly for new-to-task, monthly for veterans); major decisions sit at the lowest competent level; OKRs would remain the same without pay attached; and the manager personally taught the team something this quarter. For each negative answer, perform the listed corrective action, including defining output and building 4–6 indicators around it, pairing measures, finding and scheduling around the queue bottleneck, returning agenda ownership to reports, naming decision roles, decoupling OKRs from compensation, and scheduling manager-taught training.
