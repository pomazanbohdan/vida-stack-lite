# Design a mastery path

Mastery is the desire to improve at something that matters and is an asymptotic pursuit, not a destination. Treat ability as developable and frame failure as learning rather than judgment; mastery requires effort and deliberate practice. Calibrate challenge to skill because flow lies between boredom and anxiety; support flow with clear goals, immediate feedback, challenge-skill balance, and a sense of control. Offer a next level or challenge, show skill development, adapt difficulty, and provide clear feedback. Flat, non-adaptive difficulty and punishing failure violate mastery. When designing progress, difficulty, or feedback, consult references/mastery.md for flow calibration, deliberate practice, and the audit checklist.
