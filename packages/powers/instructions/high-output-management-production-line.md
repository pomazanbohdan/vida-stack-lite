# Manage the team as a production line

Treat every team as a production line, even when flow is not visible. Make flow visible and organize around the limiting step, scheduling other work around the longest, hardest or most expensive stage. Fix defects at the lowest-value stage before downstream work raises their cost. Batch work when setup costs are high. Most knowledge work is built to forecast rather than order: staff to the forecast and accept controlled risk. Since not all work can be watched, monitor it as a black box through a handful of indicators. Optimize systems, never treat people as interchangeable machines.
