# Price on delivered value

Price according to delivered and perceived value rather than cost, aiming for a 10:1 value-to-price ratio. The source advises anchoring against the cost of not solving the problem rather than alternatives, recognizes price as a positioning signal, and says payment plans can remove price as an objection without reducing revenue. It also states that higher prices often increase conversions and attract more committed customers who invest effort and remain longer; treat these as tendencies, not guarantees.
