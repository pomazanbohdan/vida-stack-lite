# Capture leads with consent

Capture interested prospects by exchanging a genuine lead magnet for contact information rather than expecting an immediate sale. Solve a specific immediate problem, respond within five minutes, use a CRM from day one, give the opt-in page only the contact-for-lead-magnet job, and score leads. Collect only data intended for use; never sell or share contact data without explicit opt-in consent.

# Convert leads by reducing friction

Convert nurtured leads by reducing friction, addressing objections proactively, building trust with real social proof and guarantees, and making the next step easy. Use risk reversal and a structured sales conversation. Use only real deadlines and genuine stock limits; fabricated countdowns and false scarcity are prohibited.

# Make the sale the start of the experience

Treat the sale as the start of a consistent customer experience. Map touchpoints and moments of truth, systematize delivery, build community, make personal surprises unexpected rather than expensive, measure NPS and act on it. Make cancellation as easy as signup; do not use retention mazes or hidden offboarding.

# Increase lifetime value deliberately

Increase lifetime value through retention, an ascension model, upsells, cross-sells, reactivation and appropriately timed offers. Track LTV by segment, channel and cohort rather than relying on a single average; offer expansion after a customer win.
