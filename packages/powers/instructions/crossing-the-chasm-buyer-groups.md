# Address one buyer group at a time

Distinguish five buyer groups by needs: innovators want new technology, early adopters seek vision and advantage, early majority need a complete and de-risked solution plus peer references, late majority want supported commodities, and laggards adopt only when forced. Do not market to visionaries and pragmatists simultaneously; pragmatists’ reference requirement creates a catch-22 addressed by the next steps.
