# Design the scorecard around lead generation

Design the scorecard around lead generation by using revealing questions to surface dormant desires and the tension between a prospect's current and desired reality. Prefer helping people uncover an unmet need over relying only on active searchers who have already decided and set a budget; interactive assessments are presented as more engaging than static content.
