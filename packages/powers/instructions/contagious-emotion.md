# Use high-arousal emotion

Use high-arousal emotions to drive sharing, including positive awe, excitement, amusement and inspiration or negative anger and anxiety; low-arousal contentment, relaxation and sadness suppress sharing. Focus on physiological activation rather than positive versus negative valence. Awe is described as the strongest sharing emotion. Frame facts to increase arousal when appropriate, but use negative emotion sparingly and only when the cause genuinely warrants it; outrage engineered merely for clicks corrodes trust.
