# Match go-to-market to the buyer

Execute go-to-market in a way that matches the buyer: founder-led direct sales for early adopters, proof-rich partner/channel approaches for the early majority, and lower-touch approaches for the late majority. For mainstream crossing, lead with references, whole-product completeness, ease and low risk; frame the offer as proven evolution, demonstrate ROI, and use channels pragmatists trust.
