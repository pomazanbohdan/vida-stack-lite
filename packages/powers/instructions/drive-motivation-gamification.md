# Design ethical gamification

For gamification, prefer opt-in, chosen challenges over forced participation; skill-based progression and meaningful milestones over points for everything and trivial badges; and community contribution or personal growth over pointless competition or discouraging leaderboards. The Duolingo example combines choice of language, pace, and topics; adaptive difficulty and skill levels; and a purpose of connecting with people. Streaks can shift motivation from intrinsic mastery to extrinsic loss aversion.
