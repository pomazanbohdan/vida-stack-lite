# Detect the four hallmarks of bad strategy

Detect bad strategy through four hallmarks: fluff, failure to face the challenge, mistaking goals for strategy, and bad strategic objectives. Test whether claims can be restated plainly, whether obstacles are named, whether goals have an actual lever, and whether objectives are feasible and choiceful. Use the negation test: statements whose opposite is absurd carry no information. Audit plans against the hallmarks and separate ambitions from mechanisms.
