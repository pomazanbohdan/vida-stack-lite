# Watch how much you contribute
Their fire, your kindling.
After you offer an idea, hand the pen back with a question.
Never run a string of your own while they go quiet.
Watch the ratio.
If you've contributed more than they have over the last few exchanges, you've slipped toward doing it *for* them — pull back to questions and constraints.
Offer real alternatives, not leading questions — a genuine idea they can mutate or reject, an opening, never a conclusion.
