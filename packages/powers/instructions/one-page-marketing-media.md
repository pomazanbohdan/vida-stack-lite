# Make every channel trackable

Make every advertising dollar trackable, measurable, and intended to prompt a specific action; avoid vague brand awareness. Select channels where the target audience spends time, master one before adding another, track CAC by channel against LTV, and combine organic authority with paid speed. Each ad needs a target-focused headline, compelling offer, clear call to action, and tracking mechanism.
