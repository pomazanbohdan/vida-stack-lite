# State a defensible unique selling proposition

Answer why a buyer should choose the business over its nearest competitor with a defensible, provable USP, not merely a slogan. Build it around specialization, a unique mechanism, a bold guarantee, a proprietary process, or an underserved niche; keep an elevator pitch under 30 seconds and apply the swap test for generic messaging.
