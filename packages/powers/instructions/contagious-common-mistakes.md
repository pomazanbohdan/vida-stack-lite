# Avoid the listed word-of-mouth mistakes

Avoid focusing only on online sharing, making shareable content that is not linked to its brand, using low-arousal emotions, leaving product usage invisible, relying on product quality alone, or prioritizing rare powerful triggers over frequent ones. Design for conversation triggers, apply the Trojan Horse test, use high-arousal framing, make usage observable, deliberately engineer two or three STEPPS into the experience, and prioritize trigger frequency over strength.
