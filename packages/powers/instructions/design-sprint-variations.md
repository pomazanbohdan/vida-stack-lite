# Use the sprint variations

The 4-day variation combines Map and Sketch on day one, then Decide, Prototype, and Test on days two through four. A remote sprint retains the schedule and uses Miro or FigJam whiteboards and Zoom. A multi-sprint sequence uses sprint one to choose broad direction, sprint two to explore the chosen solution, and sprint three to refine details.
