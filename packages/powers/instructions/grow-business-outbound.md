# Manufacture pipeline with outbound

Phase 3 builds a controllable outbound pipeline. Use Seeds (referrals: highest conversion, slow to build), Nets (inbound: medium), and Spears (outbound: predictable, controllable volume); most underinvest in Spears. Specialize SDR prospecting, AE closing, and CSM retention; never combine prospecting and closing in one person. Cold Calling 2.0 emails above the decision-maker for a referral in, without pitching; cited response is 9-15% versus 1-3%. Qualify with ANUM (Authority, Need, Urgency, Money) and calculate pipeline backward from revenue goal. Ask for backward email/SDR math, four-touch sequence, ANUM handoff, goal/hiring/role and channel-mix decisions. Extend MARKETING.md with process and math; done only when math, sequence, qualification/handoff and decisions are recorded.
