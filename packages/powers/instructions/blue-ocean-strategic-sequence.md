# Validate utility, price, cost and adoption in order

Validate an idea in strict sequence: exceptional buyer utility, accessible strategic price, profitable target cost, then adoption hurdles. Rework before advancing if a gate fails; set target cost from price minus target margin without sacrificing utility or deferring cost work. Identify resisting employees, partners, the public, or regulators early and address their concerns rather than steamrolling them.
