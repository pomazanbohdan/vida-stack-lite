# Identify real competitive alternatives

Identify all alternatives best customers would use if the product disappeared, including direct competitors, manual processes, spreadsheets, hiring, and doing nothing. Interview 15–20 happy customers about what they switched from; group similar alternatives and account for segment differences. Base the alternatives on actual research, not assumptions.
