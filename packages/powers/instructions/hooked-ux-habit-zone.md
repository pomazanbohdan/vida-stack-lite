# Assess the Habit Zone

A product can enter the Habit Zone when it combines high perceived value with high frequency of engagement. High perceived value at low frequency is viable but needs advertising or marketing; low perceived value is failure at either frequency. Assess how often users need to engage, the perceived value of each engagement, and whether frequency is high enough for automatic behavior.
