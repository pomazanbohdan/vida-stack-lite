# Assign the prototype roles

Assign at least two Makers to build prototype pieces; one Stitcher to combine them; one Writer to produce all copy; one or two Collectors to gather photos, icons, and competitor screenshots; one Interviewer to write and rehearse Friday interview script; and the Sprint Master to help where needed and maintain energy.
