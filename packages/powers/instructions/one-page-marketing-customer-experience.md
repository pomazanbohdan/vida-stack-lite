# Make the sale the start of the experience

Treat the sale as the start of a consistent customer experience. Map touchpoints and moments of truth, systematize delivery, build community, make personal surprises unexpected rather than expensive, measure NPS and act on it. Make cancellation as easy as signup; do not use retention mazes or hidden offboarding.
