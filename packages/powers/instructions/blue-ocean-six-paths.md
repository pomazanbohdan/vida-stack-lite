# Search beyond industry boundaries

Search beyond industry boundaries through six paths: alternative industries, strategic groups, buyer chains, complementary offerings, functional/emotional appeal, and time. Apply each path’s stated comparison to find unmet needs, overlooked buyers, total-experience pain, hybrid appeal, or trends’ endpoints.
