# Choose the market frame that clarifies value

Choose the market frame that makes unique value clearest: compete head-to-head, create a subcategory, or create a new category only when existing categories cannot describe the product. Weigh inherited assumptions against the education cost, test whether prospects understand the category within 30 seconds, and do not create a category merely to avoid competition.
