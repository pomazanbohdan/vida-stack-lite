# Move behaviors above the Action Line

Move behaviors above the Action Line primarily by increasing Ability through removing steps, pre-filling, defaults, templates, or wizards; this is the most reliable approach. Improve prompts by anchoring them to existing routines, preferring event-based over time-based prompts, and triggering when motivation is naturally higher. Motivation increases are unreliable; needing motivation tactics may indicate the behavior is too hard.
