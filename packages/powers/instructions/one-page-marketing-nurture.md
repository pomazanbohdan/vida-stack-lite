# Nurture leads with consistent value

Nurture leads who are not ready to buy through consistent value, education, and trust. Set expectations in the welcome sequence; use an approximate 3:1 value-to-ask cadence, email as the backbone with reinforcing channels, and behavior-based segmentation. Include one-click unsubscribe in every send and honor it immediately.
