# Use only real scarcity and urgency

Use scarcity to limit quantity and urgency to limit time, giving people who already want the offer a reason to act. Supply limits can include genuine seats, enrollment caps, or production runs; time limits can include enrollment windows or deadline-bound bonuses. Cohort capacity is identified as the most ethical scarcity; bonus scarcity can add urgency without limiting the core offer. Evergreen urgency must be tied to real events such as onboarding cohorts or seasonal cycles. Every scarcity and urgency claim must be 100% true; never reset a countdown timer or fake a sold-out state.
