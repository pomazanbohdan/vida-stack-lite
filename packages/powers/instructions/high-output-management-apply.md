# Run management as an output system

A manager's output is their organization's output plus that of neighboring organizations under their influence. Emails, meetings, reviews and decisions count only insofar as they raise combined output. Since managerial time is scarce, continually choose the activity creating the most output per hour and eliminate negative-leverage activities.
