# Design the scorecard landing page

Design the landing page to get visitors to start the questionnaire by creating curiosity and promising delivered value. Choose a concept hook that defines what visitors score themselves on; possible types are goal-oriented, pain-removal, readiness, category comparison, or knowledge. Include clarity, credibility, and connection; a stated short time commitment or bonus may reduce friction. Goal-oriented hooks are described as outperforming fear-based hooks. Never promise value the assessment does not deliver or disguise a sales pitch as results.
