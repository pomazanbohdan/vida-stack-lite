# Make the action dead simple

The action is the simplest behavior performed in anticipation of a reward, guided by the Fogg model: behavior occurs when motivation, ability, and trigger converge. Reducing friction by increasing ability is more reliable than trying to increase motivation; each extra step, field, or decision creates a drop-off point. Assess simplicity across time, money, physical effort, brain cycles, social deviance, and non-routine; simplify the anticipated behavior rather than the full task, and reduce choices because more choices slow decisions. When simplifying, do not hide cost or consequence: a one-tap purchase must still show its price and commitment.
