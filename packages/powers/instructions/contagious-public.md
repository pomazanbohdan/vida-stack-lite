# Make product use observable

Design product use to be observable so others can see it, learn from it and imitate it. Consider visible traces that remain after use, self-advertising features, or ways to make invisible usage visible. Visibility must empower users and remain under their control; keep private data, including failures, health and finances, private, and never use visibility to shame.
