# Hold price in high-stakes conversations

Phase 9 holds price in high-stakes conversations through empathy and being understood, not logic or compromise. Never split the difference; no deal is preferable to a bad deal because pressured discounts train future customers to ask. Name concerns with tactical empathy/labels, use calibrated questions to make the counterpart solve the problem, preempt objections with an accusation audit, and use Ackerman decreasing increments to precise non-round numbers, closing with a non-monetary concession. Prepare a specific conversation, accusation audit, five calibrated questions, target, decreasing increments, walk-away, non-monetary concession and Black-Swan hypotheses. Record a reusable playbook; done when next negotiation and decisions are prepared.
