# Qualify with ANUM

Qualify on Authority, Need, Urgency and Money. Signals: Authority — a decision maker or strong influencer is strong, no buying power is weak; Need — active pain and seeking solutions is strong, nice to have is weak; Urgency — this quarter with budget allocated is strong, someday is weak; Money — budget exists and is within range is strong, no budget or too expensive is weak. Structure the call as rapport for 2 minutes, set an agenda to understand the situation and assess fit, ask discovery questions incorporating ANUM for 10-15 minutes, and agree next steps; when qualified, schedule an AE demo.
