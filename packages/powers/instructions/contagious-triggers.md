# Link the product to frequent environmental cues

Link a product to environmental cues such as sights, sounds, times or routines so they remind people to talk about it. Prefer frequent cues over rare powerful ones, map the habitat where and when relevant contexts occur, and distinguish persistent triggers from temporary event triggers. Competitor moments may also be linked to the product. Build genuine, helpful associations and do not hijack sensitive contexts such as grief or health scares.
