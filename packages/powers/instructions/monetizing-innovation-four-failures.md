# Diagnose the four monetization failures

Classify struggling products by four testable failures: feature shock (features add complexity/cost without raising WTP), minivation (value priced too timidly), hidden gem (valuable byproduct lacks monetization owner), and undead (unwanted product kept alive beyond evidence). Match the cure: cut, raise, assign owner/spin out, or kill; a wrong cure worsens results. Flat WTP as features pile up signals feature shock; internal anchors or near-total wins without price pushback signal minivation; appoint owners for byproducts; set kill criteria before emotional investment. Kill products, never evidence; do not massage research to preserve a favorite.
