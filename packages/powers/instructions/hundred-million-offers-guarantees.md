# Reverse risk with a guarantee

Use guarantees to transfer purchase risk from buyer to seller and make acceptance psychologically safer. The five types are unconditional, conditional, anti-guarantee, implied, and performance-based. The source describes unconditional full refunds without questions as simplest and strongest for low-ticket offers; conditional guarantees requiring specified steps as attracting better clients; anti-guarantees such as all sales final as suitable when demand exceeds supply; and performance-based guarantees such as meeting a metric or not paying as the strongest risk reversal. Name the guarantee and multiple guarantees may be stacked to address multiple risk types. Make claims frictionless to invoke, with no fine-print traps or hoops.
