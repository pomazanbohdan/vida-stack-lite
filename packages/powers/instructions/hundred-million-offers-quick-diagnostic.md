# Run the offer diagnostic

Audit an existing offer with seven yes/no questions: does perceived value reach 10x price; is the market a starving crowd with pain, money, targetability, and growth; does the guarantee reverse risk; are there at least three named bonuses with dollar values; is there a genuine reason to act now; is the bundle difficult for a competitor to duplicate exactly; and does the name identify its audience and outcome? For each no, the source suggests adding value or revisiting Dream Outcome, switching or narrowing the market, adding risk reversal, creating objection-killing bonuses, adding truthful scarcity or a real deadline, differentiating the bundle, or renaming with MAGIC, respectively.
