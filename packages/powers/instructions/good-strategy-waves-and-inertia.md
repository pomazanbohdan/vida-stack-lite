# Ride waves and account for inertia

Use waves of change to recognize when industry rules and advantages shift; assess guideposts and the likely end state rather than hype. Diagnose routine, cultural, or proxy inertia and entropy; exploit a rival’s inertia where responding would break its economics. Serve the new need rather than manufacturing fear.
