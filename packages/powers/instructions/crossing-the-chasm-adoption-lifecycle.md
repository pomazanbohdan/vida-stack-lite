# Place buyers on the adoption lifecycle

The adoption lifecycle runs from innovators through early adopters, a chasm, early majority, late majority, and laggards. Pragmatists need proven, working solutions, peer references, present value, and low risk, unlike visionaries. Do not market to both groups simultaneously; the reference catch-22 is why crossing requires deliberately creating early-majority proof.
