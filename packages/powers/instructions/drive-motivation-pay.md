# Pay enough, then use now-that rewards

Pay people enough to take money off the table, fairly and ideally above-market, then focus on autonomy, mastery, and purpose; beyond enough, more money does not increase motivation. Prefer unexpected now-that rewards given after the fact over if-then rewards promised for hitting a target, because if-then rewards create pressure and short-term thinking. When applying AMP to compensation, consult references/applications.md for worked examples and escalation tables.
