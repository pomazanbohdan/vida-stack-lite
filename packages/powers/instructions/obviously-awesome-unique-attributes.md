# List only unique and true attributes

List every feature, capability, company characteristic, or approach that the product has and alternatives lack; each attribute must be both unique and true. Apply the “only we” test, distinguish product facts from customer benefits, cluster related attributes, and never claim an attribute a competitor also has.
