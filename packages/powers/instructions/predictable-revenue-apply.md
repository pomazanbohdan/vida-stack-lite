# Build predictable revenue from separated prospecting and closing

Make predictable lead generation the basis for predictable revenue. Separate prospecting from closing to create a repeatable, scalable machine; use Cold Calling 2.0 (mass personalized cold emails generating referrals to the right person) instead of traditional cold calling.
