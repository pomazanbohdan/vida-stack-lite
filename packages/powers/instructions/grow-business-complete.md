# Close or route the journey

At completion, verify MARKETING.md has nine scored squares, outbound, STEPPS and two-sided referral; OFFER.md has scored offer, persuasion audit and price-holding playbook; STRATEGY.md has beachhead/whole-product checklist and CUSTOMER.md pragmatist segment; METRICS.md has OMTM, counter-metric and line in sand; tracker has every phase done or skipped with reason and Key Decisions. Close by carrying Next Actions into artifacts as owned checkboxes. Before adding pipeline, confirm Phase 8 stickiness holds; if fundamentals (retention, churn, operational drag) are broken, continue improve-business. If product itself must carry growth loops, continue grow-app.
