# Systematize referrals and partnerships

Systematize referrals and partnerships instead of leaving them to chance: ask happy customers at peak satisfaction, make sharing easy, reward both parties, and use complementary partnerships. Disclose paid incentives and reward referrals, never fabricated reviews.
