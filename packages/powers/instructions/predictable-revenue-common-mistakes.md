# Avoid the listed sales-process mistakes

Avoid the listed practices and apply their paired fixes: AEs prospecting (hire dedicated SDRs); long, pitchy emails (use short referral-focused emails); no ICP definition (define the ICP before hiring SDRs); too few SDRs (work backward from the revenue goal); no handoff process (standardize SDR-to-AE handoff); and measuring activity instead of results (track qualified opportunities, not emails).
