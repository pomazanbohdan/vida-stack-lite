# Run the AE role

An AE closes deals from qualified pipeline by conducting discovery, demos, negotiation and closing, then handing the account to a CSM. AEs do not prospect, qualify inbound leads, or manage post-sale accounts. Measure revenue closed, win rate, average deal size, and sales cycle length.
