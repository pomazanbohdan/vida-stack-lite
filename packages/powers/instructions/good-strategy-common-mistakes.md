# Avoid common strategy mistakes

Avoid goals masquerading as strategy, skipped diagnosis, template boilerplate, untestable fluff, refusal to choose, excessive or blue-sky objectives, evenly spread resources and treating strategy as settled truth. Replace them with a kernel, a critical challenge, explicit choices, a small set of proximate objectives, concentrated effort and evidence-based review.
