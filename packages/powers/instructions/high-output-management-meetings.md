# Run process meetings and 1:1s

Use regular process meetings, including 1:1s, staff meetings, and operation reviews, to carry most managerial work, at roughly one quarter of the calendar; mission-oriented meetings are ad hoc and exist to produce a decision. If ad hoc mission meetings consume more than about one quarter of managerial time, treat the process as broken. The subordinate owns and brings the 1:1 agenda; the supervisor listens, takes notes of agreed actions, and asks what else is on the subordinate's mind after the agenda. Set 1:1 frequency by task-relevant maturity, not seniority or affection: weekly for new-to-task, every few weeks for veterans, and never less often than monthly. Both parties keep a hold list for non-urgent topics. Run staff meetings as controlled free discussion with the manager moderating as a Socratic prompter; recurring ad hoc meetings are process meetings in denial. Do not turn 1:1s into status extraction; put status in writing.
