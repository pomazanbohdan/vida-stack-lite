# Run the sticky decision

Display sketches for silent review and dot-mark interesting parts. Discuss each sketch for three minutes while the facilitator narrates and the anonymous sketcher stays silent; a scribe records standout ideas on the whiteboard. Have each person cast a non-binding vote for one solution with one sentence of rationale. The Decider receives three large dots and the Supervote decision wins.
