# Build persuasion into every surface

Phase 5 improves conversion on existing-traffic surfaces (pricing page, testimonials, checkout) without more acquisition spend. Audit Reciprocity, Commitment and Consistency, Social Proof, Authority, Liking, Scarcity and Unity. Prefer exact numbers to vague claims; negative social proof backfires; admitting weakness can raise authority; newly scarce framing is stronger than always-scarce. Persuasion helps people see value they would appreciate; manipulation works against their interests. Audit presence/misses, score, rewrite weak spots and ethics-check every tactic. Replace any tactic failing ethics review with one based on evidence available; verify all scarcity and proof claims are real. Extend OFFER.md with audit and concrete testimonials placed where objections peak; done when scored audit, rewritten proof and verified true claims are recorded.
