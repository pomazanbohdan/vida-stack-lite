# Design the investment

Investment is a user contribution—time, data, effort, social capital, or money—that improves the product for the next use, raises switching costs, and loads the next trigger. It should follow reward rather than precede it, because it improves the next cycle rather than providing immediate reward. Each investment should load the next trigger, and small investments can compound into stored value and better personalization. Investment must genuinely improve the experience; do not trap users with artificial switching costs or impossible data export. Make staying the better choice through real value.
