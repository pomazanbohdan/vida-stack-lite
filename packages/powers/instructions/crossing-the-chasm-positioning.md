# Position for a target customer and need

Position for a target customer and need by naming the product category, key benefit, primary alternative, and primary differentiation. The alternative may be a manual process, spreadsheet, or legacy system rather than a direct competitor. Differentiate on a dimension you dominate and make the incumbent’s strength irrelevant.
