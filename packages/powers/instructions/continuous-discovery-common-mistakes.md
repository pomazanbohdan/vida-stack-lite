# Avoid common discovery mistakes

Avoid phase-based or PM-only discovery, jumping from outcomes to solutions, asking customers what they want, testing easy rather than risky assumptions, isolated opportunity scoring, and interview bursts; use weekly trio discovery, story interviews, risk-based testing, comparisons, and recurring recruitment.
