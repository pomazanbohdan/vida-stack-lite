# Create social currency

Create social currency by making sharing help people appear smart, cool or informed and by giving them insider status or identity value. Use remarkable information, visible accomplishments such as leaderboards/badges/status tiers, genuine exclusivity or scarcity, or an unexpected angle on an otherwise ordinary product. Create real insider value; do not use false scarcity or manufactured exclusivity that breeds toxicity.
