# Choose work by managerial leverage

Managerial leverage is output per unit of managerial time. Favor work affecting many people or redirecting substantial work, such as training, prepared decisions and information gathering. Avoid negative leverage from meddling with experts, delaying decisions and visible gloom. Delegate tasks known best by the manager; delegation without monitoring is abdication. Monitor tasks rather than people, sampling deeper at low task-relevant maturity and lighter as it rises. Schedule 1:1s, staff meetings, reviews and planning first, fitting interrupts around them. Operate below full load so surprises do not delay downstream work; decline work early when full. Batch interruptions with office hours or checkpoints. Do not hoard information or approvals into a bottleneck.
