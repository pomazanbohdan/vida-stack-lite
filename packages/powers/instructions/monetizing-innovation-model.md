# Choose the monetization model and price metric

Choose the price metric before price level; select a metric tracking delivered value and a model fitting customer consumption and payment. Treat freemium as conversion-oriented acquisition spend, not the pricing model or generosity program. Manage usage-price volatility and bill shock with caps, alerts or committed tiers. Account for per-seat pricing taxing collaboration and outcome pricing requiring trusted attribution. Consider a hybrid platform fee plus usage for a predictable floor and value-linked upside. A model migration reprices existing customers at once: grandfather generously and lead with value. Choose metrics customers can predict and audit; surprise bills monetize confusion, not value.
