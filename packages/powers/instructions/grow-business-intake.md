# Run intake before the tracker

Before creating the tracker, ask about revenue, customer count and monthly lumpiness; sources and dominant channel; candidate segments; current offer, price and willingness-to-pay testing; product network dimension; prior customer, positioning and strategy artifacts; and the most painful current growth constraint. These answers provide Phase 8 baselines, Phase 3 volume, Phase 1 square/PVP and Phase 6 beachhead inputs, gate optional phases, avoid re-derivation, and may justify leading with Phase 2. Skip Phase 3 for pure self-serve without desired sales assistance; Phase 6 if already selling to the mainstream pragmatist majority; Phase 7 absent a multiplayer/network dimension; Phase 9 for fixed self-serve pricing without negotiated deals. Then create tracker from template, status every phase, and confirm plan.
