# Choose the target customer and moment

Choose the customer and moment on the map representing the biggest risk or opportunity as the week focus; the Decider with authority makes the final call. Monday output includes the long-term goal, sprint questions, journey map, expert insights, organized HMW notes, and target customer and moment.
