# Research willingness to pay before building

Research WTP while the product is a concept, before specs freeze, business-case lock or coding, to learn whether customers value the idea, how much and which parts, rather than set a final price. Ask acceptable, expensive and prohibitively expensive price points; on a 1-5 purchase-probability scale count only discounted top-box 5s as positive, 4s as maybes and lower as no. Prefer feature ranking or priced-bundle trade-offs to direct questions. Frame the discussion as value research, never a quote or negotiation. Do not justify building a feature without stating its WTP range. Rebuild the living business case whenever scope, segment or price assumptions move, weekly rather than annually. Match price to delivered value, not each customer's maximum pain.
