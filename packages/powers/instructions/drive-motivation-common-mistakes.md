# Avoid the listed motivation mistakes

Avoid awarding points for everything; reserve rewards for meaningful milestones. Make participation opt-in. Match challenge to individuals with adaptive difficulty. Make progress visible through progress indicators or skill tracking. Connect every feature to its purpose. For if-then bonuses, pay fairly, use now-that rewards, and focus on autonomy, mastery, and purpose.
