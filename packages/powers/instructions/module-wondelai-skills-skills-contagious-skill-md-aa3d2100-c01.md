# Avoid the listed word-of-mouth mistakes

Avoid focusing only on online sharing, making shareable content that is not linked to its brand, using low-arousal emotions, leaving product usage invisible, relying on product quality alone, or prioritizing rare powerful triggers over frequent ones. Design for conversation triggers, apply the Trojan Horse test, use high-arousal framing, make usage observable, deliberately engineer two or three STEPPS into the experience, and prioritize trigger frequency over strength.

# Run the STEPPS diagnostic

Use the six Quick Diagnostic questions to assess whether sharing enhances the sharer, an everyday cue recalls the product, it evokes high-arousal emotion, use is visible, it is useful enough to forward, and the brand is embedded in a retellable story. For each no, apply the listed action: add remarkability/exclusivity/achievements; link a frequent context or routine; reframe for awe/excitement/humor/righteous anger; add observable signals or branded outputs; package tips/lists/tools for sharing; or create a brand-dependent Trojan Horse story.

# Use high-arousal emotion

Use high-arousal emotions to drive sharing, including positive awe, excitement, amusement and inspiration or negative anger and anxiety; low-arousal contentment, relaxation and sadness suppress sharing. Focus on physiological activation rather than positive versus negative valence. Awe is described as the strongest sharing emotion. Frame facts to increase arousal when appropriate, but use negative emotion sparingly and only when the cause genuinely warrants it; outrage engineered merely for clicks corrodes trust.

# Use the launch phase combinations

For a product launch, the suggested phase combinations are: pre-launch Social Currency plus Public; launch day Emotion plus Stories; first week Triggers plus Practical Value; sustained growth Public plus Social Currency. Example tactics are an invite-only beta with visible waitlist, founder narrative with an awe-inducing demo, daily-workflow tie with share-to-unlock, and visible usage with achievement sharing, respectively.
