# Distinguish red and blue oceans

Red oceans are existing markets where rivals compete over shrinking profits; blue oceans are new spaces where competition is irrelevant. The comparison requires creating demand and pursuing differentiation and low cost together rather than exploiting existing demand or choosing one.
