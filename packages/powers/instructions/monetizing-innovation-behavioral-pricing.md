# Price with behavioral science and communicate it

Use anchors, compromise effect, decoys and price endings to frame choice, with disciplined communication protecting price. Measure a decoy by whether it shifts choices, not whether it sells. Match charm or round endings to deal or quality position. Announce increases with delivered value before specifics and ample notice; do not apologize and discount together. Diagnose awareness, channel and packaging before changing price; price is last lever. Set day-30/60/90 triggers before launch and monitor instead of panic-discounting. Tactics must frame real value; false claims make anchors, decoys and endings deceptive.
