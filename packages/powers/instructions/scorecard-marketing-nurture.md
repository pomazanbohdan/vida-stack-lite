# Promote and follow up by score tier

Promote the scorecard through suitable channels; send the results email and PDF immediately after completion and use abandonment emails to recover incomplete starts. Segment nurture campaigns by score tier. For high-ticket funnels, use sequential stages: 8–15 questions with a basic score, 15–25 with a detailed report, then 30–50 with a baseline and roadmap. Do not weaponize self-reported pain points to pressure prospects; use volunteered data to tailor help, not manufacture urgency.
