# Treat strategy as a testable hypothesis

Treat strategy as a hypothesis: diagnose, formulate, test against evidence, and revise. Generate genuinely different alternatives, attack the front-runner, ask what else could explain the situation, use a virtual panel for critique rather than staged endorsement, and keep the written kernel beside an explicit no-list. Independent judgment matters when consensus conflicts with evidence.
