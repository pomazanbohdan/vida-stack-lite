# Avoid the listed management mistakes

Do not measure activity instead of output; count shipped work that survives and pair quantity with quality before publishing indicators. When busy, reschedule 1:1s but never drop them. Avoid rank-based decisions; discuss freely and let the named decider decide. Keep OKRs as a stretch tool rather than a compensation formula. Match management style to TRM task by task. Inspect specifications and plans before production so defects are caught at a lower-cost stage. Deliver feedback when events happen rather than saving it for an annual review.
