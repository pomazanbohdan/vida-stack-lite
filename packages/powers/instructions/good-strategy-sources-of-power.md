# Apply strength at the point of leverage

Apply strength where it has greatest effect through leverage, proximate objectives, chain-link systems, coordinated design, focus, and protected advantage. Anticipate behavior, find a pivot point, and concentrate; choose objectives close enough to hit, fix the weakest link before strengthening others, coordinate tightly when stakes are high and resources scarce, and deepen or protect advantage at the point of competition. Build isolating mechanisms on delivered value, not lock-in designed only to trap users.
