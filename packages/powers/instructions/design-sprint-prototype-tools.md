# Choose prototype tools and build in two halves

Select tools suited to the product: linked Figma, Keynote, or PowerPoint slides for web or apps; video walkthrough or 3D-printed mockup for physical products; or role-play video or scripted interaction for services. In the morning, divide storyboard scenes among makers; in the afternoon, stitch the prototype, check it against the storyboard, rehearse the full flow, and trial it with someone outside the sprint team. Ensure it follows the storyboard exactly, looks real enough for honest reactions, takes 5-15 minutes to walk through, can be presented by the interviewer, and has completed a trial run. Thursday output is the realistic prototype, interview script, and prepared interview room.
