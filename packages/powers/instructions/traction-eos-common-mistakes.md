# Avoid the listed EOS mistakes

Avoid skipping Level 10 meetings: protect the weekly rhythm and never cancel. Avoid operating without a scorecard: choose 5-15 weekly numbers. Address wrong-person decisions with the People Analyzer and make difficult calls. Share the V/TO with the entire company so the team knows the vision. For rock-specific mistakes (too many, too vague, business-as-usual, or mid-quarter changes), consult references/rocks.md.
