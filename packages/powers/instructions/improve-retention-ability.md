# Fix the weakest Ability Chain link

Ability is determined by the scarcest resource across Time, Money, Physical Effort, Mental Effort, Social Deviance, and Non-Routine; a sufficiently weak link breaks the behavior. Diagnose and fix the weakest link rather than the most obvious factor. Starter Steps shrink behavior to its tiniest version; defaults can reduce effort. Reduce friction only for genuinely valuable behaviors, and never make overspending, oversharing, or deletion of important data too easy without confirmation.
