# Choose Rumble or All-in-One

If multiple sketches win, choose either Rumble, with competing prototypes testing different approaches, or All-in-One, combining the best ideas into one prototype. Draw a simple 10-15 panel comic of the test experience from customer discovery through solution use to successful outcome; make the UI specific and include enough detail for Thursday prototype. Wednesday output is the winning solution or solutions and a detailed prototype-ready storyboard.
