# Capture leads with consent

Capture interested prospects by exchanging a genuine lead magnet for contact information rather than expecting an immediate sale. Solve a specific immediate problem, respond within five minutes, use a CRM from day one, give the opt-in page only the contact-for-lead-magnet job, and score leads. Collect only data intended for use; never sell or share contact data without explicit opt-in consent.
