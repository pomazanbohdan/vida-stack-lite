# Cross the chasm to the mainstream

Phase 6 moves from visionaries, who tolerate rough edges and buy vision, to pragmatist early-majority buyers who want proven solutions, peer references and a complete product. Dominate one narrow beachhead with urgent expensive pain, a reachable channel and members who communicate; assemble the whole product (integrations, onboarding, migration, support), partnering for gaps. Reposition 'revolutionary' as a proven solution for a problem and 'be first' as joining peer firms. Ask the user to confirm beachhead, build/partner gaps and pragmatist repositioning. Record beachhead and checklist in STRATEGY.md and pragmatist segment in CUSTOMER.md; done when these and decisions are present.

# Avoid the listed growth mistakes

Do not optimize a funnel before fixing the offer; apply Phase 2 first. Do not treat the nine skills as a buffet: preserve order and shared inputs (PVP niche to beachhead, USP to cold email, referral square to network loop). Reposition pragmatist marketing through Phase 6 as a proven solution. Never fake scarcity, social proof or guarantees; use real inventory, numbers and deadlines. Do not celebrate vanity metrics; Phase 8 identifies cumulative upward totals as decoration. Do not build outbound on poor retention: let Phase 8 stickiness gate acquisition spend and run improve-business first when retention is broken.

# Build a repeatable growth engine

Build a repeatable growth engine in which each phase produces an asset consumed by the next; preserve phase order because skipping Phase 1 removes the shared target. Ask decision questions, record answers, and invoke constituent skills instead of improvising their methods. Fix the offer in Phase 2 before activating outbound, virality, repositioning, or any channel, because traffic to a weak offer is costly.

# Run intake before the tracker

Before creating the tracker, ask about revenue, customer count and monthly lumpiness; sources and dominant channel; candidate segments; current offer, price and willingness-to-pay testing; product network dimension; prior customer, positioning and strategy artifacts; and the most painful current growth constraint. These answers provide Phase 8 baselines, Phase 3 volume, Phase 1 square/PVP and Phase 6 beachhead inputs, gate optional phases, avoid re-derivation, and may justify leading with Phase 2. Skip Phase 3 for pure self-serve without desired sales assistance; Phase 6 if already selling to the mainstream pragmatist majority; Phase 7 absent a multiplayer/network dimension; Phase 9 for fixed self-serve pricing without negotiated deals. Then create tracker from template, status every phase, and confirm plan.

# Install the marketing operating system

Phase 1 maps marketing across a 3x3 Before (target market, message, media), During (capture leads, nurture, convert), and After (deliver experience, increase lifetime value, orchestrate referrals) grid. Score completeness and specificity of all nine squares 0-10, identify the weakest and three fixes, and run the PVP Index (Personal fulfillment, Value to market, Profitability) across candidate segments to choose a niche and write its avatar. Ask the user to choose the PVP niche, weakest square, and one named persona, not everyone. Create MARKETING.md with target market/avatar, the grid and channels; update tracker. Done only when grid is filled/scored, niche/avatar recorded, and user has chosen niche and square.
