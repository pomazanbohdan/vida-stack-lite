# Avoid the listed offer mistakes

Avoid selling a commodity by bundling unique value into a category of one; price against Dream Outcome value rather than cost; reverse the prospect's risk with a guarantee; name each bonus and describe its value and dollar amount; use only real, verifiable scarcity; apply MAGIC instead of generic naming; and narrow an audience that is too broad.
