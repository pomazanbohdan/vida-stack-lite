# Avoid common marketing mistakes

Avoid broad targeting, price-only competition, untracked awareness ads, sending all traffic to the home page, neglecting existing customers, and missing follow-up; apply niche selection, a genuine USP, direct-response tracking, lead magnets and dedicated landing pages, AFTER-stage systems, and CRM nurture.
