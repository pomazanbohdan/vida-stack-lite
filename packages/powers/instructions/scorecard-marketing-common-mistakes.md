# Avoid the listed scorecard mistakes

Avoid these failure patterns and apply the stated fixes: for cold traffic, keep to 8–15 questions and save 20–50 for warm leads because abandonment rises after 15; capture email before the quiz; provide unique dynamic content for each tier and category; frame questions around the respondent rather than the offer; give each results page a specific tier-appropriate CTA; segment follow-up by score tier; and test 3–5 concept hooks with the audience.
