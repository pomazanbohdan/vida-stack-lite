# Convert leads by reducing friction

Convert nurtured leads by reducing friction, addressing objections proactively, building trust with real social proof and guarantees, and making the next step easy. Use risk reversal and a structured sales conversation. Use only real deadlines and genuine stock limits; fabricated countdowns and false scarcity are prohibited.
