# Connect work to purpose

Purpose is acting in service of something larger than oneself and provides context for autonomy and mastery; without it, autonomy is directionless and mastery hollow. Express purpose through goals, words, and policies. In products, show user impact, connect people to a larger community, and align with values. Show aggregate impact, connect individual actions to collective outcomes, and celebrate meaningful milestones over vanity metrics. When wiring impact, community, or values features, consult references/purpose.md for Goals/Words/Policies patterns and the purpose audit checklist.
