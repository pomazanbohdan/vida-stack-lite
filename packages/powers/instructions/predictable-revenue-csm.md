# Run the CSM role

A CSM retains and grows accounts by onboarding customers, driving adoption, surfacing expansion opportunities, and preventing churn. Measure net revenue retention, churn rate, expansion revenue, and NPS/CSAT. The intended cycle connects SDR pipeline generation, AE closing, CSM retention and growth, and referrals from happy customers.
