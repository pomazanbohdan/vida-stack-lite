# Avoid the listed growth mistakes

Do not optimize a funnel before fixing the offer; apply Phase 2 first. Do not treat the nine skills as a buffet: preserve order and shared inputs (PVP niche to beachhead, USP to cold email, referral square to network loop). Reposition pragmatist marketing through Phase 6 as a proven solution. Never fake scarcity, social proof or guarantees; use real inventory, numbers and deadlines. Do not celebrate vanity metrics; Phase 8 identifies cumulative upward totals as decoration. Do not build outbound on poor retention: let Phase 8 stickiness gate acquisition spend and run improve-business first when retention is broken.
