# Apply the Value Equation

Model perceived value as (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) divided by (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice); increase the numerator and reduce the denominator. Define the outcome from what customers want, account for time, comfort, status, and identity sacrifices, and use social proof, guarantees, or track record as evidence that may raise perceived likelihood. A guarantee can also lower perceived risk. Back every speed, effort, and results claim with data, or clearly label it aspirational instead of asserting it.
