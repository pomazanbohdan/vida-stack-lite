# Package features into tiers

Classify every feature as leader, filler or killer. Build good-better-best tiers around leaders, use fillers for modest value/differentiation, and unbundle or remove killers. Killer value is segment-relative, not inherently bad. Withhold the full leader from the lowest tier while leaving a taste; design the middle tier first. Target roughly 70/20/10 across middle/premium/entry; investigate weak fences if most choose bottom and possible minivation if most choose top. Bundle complementary components when they raise total WTP; unbundle when segments diverge or a killer enters. Default to three tiers, maximum four. Fence on added value, never by withholding essentials: security, privacy and data export belong in every tier.
