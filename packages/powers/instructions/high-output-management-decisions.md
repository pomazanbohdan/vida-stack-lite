# Make decisions with explicit roles and stretch OKRs

Make decisions through free discussion with dissent welcome, a crisp stated decision (crisper when more contentious), and full support after disagreement. Decide at the lowest competent level closest to current technical knowledge. Planning assesses environmental demand, faces current status honestly, and closes the gap through decisions and actions now rather than documents; plan for next year's gap rather than this week's firefighting. Break peer-group indecision with a peer-plus-one: a sanctioned senior participant who can tip the decision. Before every decision meeting, specify what decision is needed, by when, who decides, who is consulted, who ratifies or vetoes, and who is informed. If no one person has both needed forms of authority, pair the freshest technical knowledge with the strongest organizational judgment. Reverse decisions openly when new facts arise; quiet reversals are waffling. Use objectives to state where to go and measurable key results to track progress; keep objectives few, make results scorable without argument, cascade each level's key results into the next level's objectives, and never mechanically tie OKRs to compensation.
