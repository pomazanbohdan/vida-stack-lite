# Resolve issues with IDS

Use IDS (Identify, Discuss, Solve) to resolve issues: identify root cause by asking why until it is reached and state the issue in one sentence; discuss with input from everyone (not equal time), stop tangents, and address one issue at a time in a 5-15 minute time box; solve by deciding, assigning who/what/when actions, and moving on. Apply IDS to problems, ideas (decide yes/no/later), and obstacles (remove or escalate). Anyone may add issues; prioritize the most important first, and carry unsolved issues forward. Avoid symptom-only discussion, repeating the same issue weekly, and ending without clear actions. Consult the issues reference when facilitating.
