# Support autonomy over task, time, technique and team

Autonomy is the desire to direct one's own life through choice over what, when, how, and with whom; it is not independence, since people can choose while interdependent with a team. In product design, enable choice through task, time, technique, and team; examples include selectable onboarding paths, user-controlled feeds, and customizable workflows. Forced prerequisites, unskippable tutorials, mandatory notifications, and single forced paths violate autonomy. When designing onboarding, feeds, or workflow controls, consult references/autonomy.md for Four T's patterns and the audit checklist.
