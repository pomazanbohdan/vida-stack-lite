# Cross the chasm to the mainstream

Phase 6 moves from visionaries, who tolerate rough edges and buy vision, to pragmatist early-majority buyers who want proven solutions, peer references and a complete product. Dominate one narrow beachhead with urgent expensive pain, a reachable channel and members who communicate; assemble the whole product (integrations, onboarding, migration, support), partnering for gaps. Reposition 'revolutionary' as a proven solution for a problem and 'be first' as joining peer firms. Ask the user to confirm beachhead, build/partner gaps and pragmatist repositioning. Record beachhead and checklist in STRATEGY.md and pragmatist segment in CUSTOMER.md; done when these and decisions are present.
