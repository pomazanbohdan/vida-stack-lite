# Build the strategy kernel

A strategy requires all three kernel elements: diagnosis of the critical challenge, a guiding policy for overcoming diagnosed obstacles, and coherent coordinated actions that carry out the policy; a document missing any element is not yet a strategy. Diagnose before prescribing; the policy should channel effort and rule out options, and actions should reinforce one another with an owner, resources, and date. A one-page kernel needs no mission/vision/values preamble. An honest diagnosis includes internal causes and should not be softened to protect egos or politics.
