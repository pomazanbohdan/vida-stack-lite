# Embed the brand in a retellable story

Embed the brand in a retellable narrative so the brand travels with the story like a Trojan Horse. A story fails this test if it can be retold without the brand; it must also survive casual conversation without a long setup. A funny ad that is not attributed to the brand is a failure. Stories must be true or clearly fictional; fabricated testimonials and origins damage future word of mouth.
