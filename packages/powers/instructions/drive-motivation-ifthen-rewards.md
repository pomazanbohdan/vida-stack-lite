# Avoid if-then rewards for creative work

If-then rewards can extinguish intrinsic motivation, diminish performance, crush creativity, crowd out good behavior, encourage cheating, become addictive, and foster short-term thinking. Extrinsic rewards work only for routine, algorithmic tasks with no intrinsic interest; they backfire for creative work, complex problem-solving, or long-term engagement. When a reward or incentive scheme is backfiring, consult references/extrinsic-rewards.md for the named studies and the decision rule for safe reward use.
