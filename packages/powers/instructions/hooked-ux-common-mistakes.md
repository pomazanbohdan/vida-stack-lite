# Avoid the listed Hook Model mistakes

Avoid relying indefinitely on external triggers; map the product to an emotion and transition to internal triggers within 30 days. Avoid an overly complex core action; simplify it to the minimum viable action and apply the six ability factors. Avoid predictable rewards; add variability across Tribe, Hunt, and Self rewards. Ask for investment only after users receive reward, sequencing Trigger → Action → Reward → Investment. Do not ignore hook ethics; use the Manipulation Matrix and be a Facilitator, not a Dealer.
