# Use the launch phase combinations

For a product launch, the suggested phase combinations are: pre-launch Social Currency plus Public; launch day Emotion plus Stories; first week Triggers plus Practical Value; sustained growth Public plus Social Currency. Example tactics are an invite-only beta with visible waitlist, founder narrative with an awe-inducing demo, daily-workflow tie with share-to-unlock, and visible usage with achievement sharing, respectively.
