# Re-pitch in plain words
Wait, I don't understand where you've got to here. Re-pitch that: give me a little bit of context, talk in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English, and use the ubiquitous language from the project glossary (follow the glossary map to the right one if the repo has more than one).
