# Run three-minute lightning demos

Use three-minute demos of competitor and analogous products; presenters say what they found and why it is interesting, and useful ideas are captured on the whiteboard. Borrow ideas from any industry. Divide the map among people if it has multiple parts; otherwise have everyone tackle the same critical problem.
