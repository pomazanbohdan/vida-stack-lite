# Align the team on positioning

Align founders, product, sales, marketing and customer success through pre-work using customer research and win/loss data, a 2–3 hour workshop covering all five steps, and post-work documenting the canvas and aligning customer-facing material. The required outcome is that everyone describes the product consistently.
