# Avoid the listed pricing mistakes

Avoid building before pricing, cost-plus or competitor-copy pricing, direct 'would you buy this?' questions, average-WTP design, one-size offers, bundling killers, freemium as business model and panic-discounting. Instead test concept WTP, price from validated ranges, probe acceptable/expensive/prohibitive price and use forced trade-offs, segment the WTP curve, match three or four offers to WTP clusters, unbundle or cut killers, use free as acquisition capped at habit point with leader gated, and pre-set triggers while diagnosing awareness and packaging.
