# Run the AE role

An AE closes deals from qualified pipeline by conducting discovery, demos, negotiation and closing, then handing the account to a CSM. AEs do not prospect, qualify inbound leads, or manage post-sale accounts. Measure revenue closed, win rate, average deal size, and sales cycle length.

# Qualify with ANUM

Qualify on Authority, Need, Urgency and Money. Signals: Authority — a decision maker or strong influencer is strong, no buying power is weak; Need — active pain and seeking solutions is strong, nice to have is weak; Urgency — this quarter with budget allocated is strong, someday is weak; Money — budget exists and is within range is strong, no budget or too expensive is weak. Structure the call as rapport for 2 minutes, set an agenda to understand the situation and assess fit, ask discovery questions incorporating ANUM for 10-15 minutes, and agree next steps; when qualified, schedule an AE demo.

# Build predictable revenue from separated prospecting and closing

Make predictable lead generation the basis for predictable revenue. Separate prospecting from closing to create a repeatable, scalable machine; use Cold Calling 2.0 (mass personalized cold emails generating referrals to the right person) instead of traditional cold calling.

# Avoid the listed sales-process mistakes

Avoid the listed practices and apply their paired fixes: AEs prospecting (hire dedicated SDRs); long, pitchy emails (use short referral-focused emails); no ICP definition (define the ICP before hiring SDRs); too few SDRs (work backward from the revenue goal); no handoff process (standardize SDR-to-AE handoff); and measuring activity instead of results (track qualified opportunities, not emails).

# Run the CSM role

A CSM retains and grows accounts by onboarding customers, driving adoption, surfacing expansion opportunities, and preventing churn. Measure net revenue retention, churn rate, expansion revenue, and NPS/CSAT. The intended cycle connects SDR pipeline generation, AE closing, CSM retention and growth, and referrals from happy customers.

# Run the predictable-revenue diagnostic

Audit a B2B sales process with the five questions: whether prospecting and closing are separated, whether outbound has a defined process, whether pipeline can be predicted three months ahead, whether lead-type mix is known, and whether SDR-to-AE handoff is standardized. For each 'No', use the paired action: create a dedicated SDR role, implement Cold Calling 2.0, build a pipeline math model, balance seeds/nets/spears, or create a handoff checklist, respectively.
