# Install EOS in the staged sequence

A typical full EOS implementation takes two years. The sequence is Focus Day on day 1 (8 hours) for the accountability chart, rocks, scorecard, and Level 10; Vision Building Day 1 in month 1 for core values, core focus, and 10-year target; Vision Building Day 2 in month 2 for marketing strategy, 3-year, 1-year, and rocks; quarterly sessions every 90 days to review and set rocks and address major issues; and annual planning yearly for full V/TO review, 1-year plan, and Q1 rocks. Self-implementation is free and slower; a certified EOS Implementer is faster and expensive. Consult references/implementation.md for phase sequencing and the self-versus-Implementer decision, and references/case-studies.md for real-company sequencing examples.
