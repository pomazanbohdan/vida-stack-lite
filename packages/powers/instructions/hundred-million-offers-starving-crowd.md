# Find a starving crowd

Choose a market with all four characteristics: massive pain, purchasing power, easy targeting, and growth. Give pain the greatest weight because people pay to stop pain faster than to gain pleasure. Easy targeting means the market is reachable through existing channels such as associations, communities, or platforms. Narrow the audience because specificity signals expertise and raises perceived value. Target genuine need and fit, never vulnerability; avoid people in crisis who cannot make rational decisions.
