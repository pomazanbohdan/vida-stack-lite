# Align the team on positioning

Align founders, product, sales, marketing and customer success through pre-work using customer research and win/loss data, a 2–3 hour workshop covering all five steps, and post-work documenting the canvas and aligning customer-facing material. The required outcome is that everyone describes the product consistently.

# Use trends only as supporting evidence

Use real, widely acknowledged trends directly connected to unique value only as supporting evidence, never as the core positioning. Treat a trend as abuse if the positioning depends on it, it connects to no unique attribute, or it is aspirational rather than occurring.

# List only unique and true attributes

List every feature, capability, company characteristic, or approach that the product has and alternatives lack; each attribute must be both unique and true. Apply the “only we” test, distinguish product facts from customer benefits, cluster related attributes, and never claim an attribute a competitor also has.

# Map attributes to customer value

For each unique attribute, repeatedly ask “So what?” until reaching customer-valued outcomes, express value in customer language, support themes with evidence, and group related values. The core concept says two or three themes, while the Key insights say most products support 2–4; retain this source conflict unresolved.
